About the App

DV Icons & Images is a self-contained, offline-first tool for preparing icons, images and banners directly on your phone. There is no server, no account and no upload — every operation starts and ends on this device.

How to Use the App

1. Open the Images tab and select a photo from your device.

2. Choose a file name, format and maximum width, then tap Prepare Image.

3. Download the result or copy it as a data URL.

4. Switch to the Banner tab to compose text and author credits over a background, then download the finished banner.
